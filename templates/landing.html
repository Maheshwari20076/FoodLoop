The Problem

Good food is going to waste — while people go hungry

Restaurants, canteens, hostels and event caterers regularly end up with surplus, edible food. Without a fast way to connect it to nearby recipients before it expires, it's thrown away instead of rescued.

Time is the enemy

Surplus food has a short safe-to-eat window, and without a fast alert system, it expires before anyone even hears about it.

No easy way to connect

Donors don't know which nearby NGO or shelter can actually use the food right now, and how much they can take.

No accountability

Without verification or tracking, food donation stays informal, unreliable, and hard to measure.

How It Works

From surplus to plate, in three steps

FoodLoop's core rescue flow is built to move fast — because every minute counts.

1
Donor Lists Surplus Food

A restaurant, canteen or caterer creates a donation with quantity, meal count, and pickup deadline.

2
Smart Rescue Matching

Our algorithm instantly scores nearby verified recipients on urgency, distance, capacity and suitability.

3
Claim & Confirm Pickup

The best-fit recipient claims it, gets a 6-digit pickup code, and confirms once the handoff happens.
